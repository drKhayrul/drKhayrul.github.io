If by Music Information Design / Music Information Retrieval (MIR) you mean companies working on:

  • Music AI

  • Music recommendation

  • Music metadata and databases

  • Music generation

  • Audio analysis

  • Sound design and sonic branding

  • Music informatics

then these are some of the most relevant organizations in Japan:

1. Yamaha Corporation

Particularly its Music Informatics and New Applications Laboratory (MINA Lab), which conducts research on music informatics, music interaction, performance analysis, and AI-assisted music creation. This is one of the closest industry groups to academic Music Information Retrieval (MIR).

2. RecoChoku : Often described as a music IT company. Works on digital music services, music data, artist platforms, recommendation technologies, and AI-driven music services.

3. Amadeus Code: One of Japan's leading music-AI companies. Develops AI composition tools, music datasets, music-generation systems, and music metadata infrastructure. Their work is closely related to MIR, generative AI, and computational musicology.

4. Japan Music Data Corporation (JMD):Maintains large-scale music metadata and catalog databases used by the Japanese music industry. This is highly relevant if your interest is music information management and music databases.

5. coton Inc.A startup originating from Tokyo University of the Arts that works at the intersection of sound, AI, cognition, well-being, and interactive sound environments.

6. INTERNET Co., Ltd. Developer of music production software, Vocaloid-related products, audio processing tools, and music technology systems.

7. Ide Sound Laboratory (井出音研究所)Focuses on sound design, auditory interfaces, AI-based sound expression, sonic branding, and sound-space design. Very relevant if your interest is information design through sound.
